# Policy/value self-play: prospective protocol

Authorized 2026-10-15. Freeze this protocol and `configs/policy-value.json` before any pilot measurements. Scientific code, configuration, input/checkpoint hashes, environment and opening manifests are sealed by the runner; resume rejects mismatches. No new labels or trained ranker weights are used. Published artifacts and default remain unchanged.

Two treatments: visit-policy cross entropy plus completed-game outcome MSE, versus the same loss plus 0.1 mean squared deviation from detached frozen initial value predictions. Policy/value trunk and value head copy the combined seed-42 128/64 v3 evaluator. Both arms and run seeds 42/43/44 share one fresh policy-head initialization (seed 20261015). Independent per-game, opening and minibatch RNG streams pair corresponding arms without assuming identical trajectories after divergence.

PUCT uses c=1.5, zero Q for unvisited edges, exploration sqrt(max(1,total visits)), legal-generator tie order, legal softmax priors, and normalized terminal returns. Each simulation completes one backup; root expansion is a separately counted inference. Trees restart at every move, with no transposition merging. Full history and automatic draw claims apply before network evaluation. Self-play root noise mixes 25% Dirichlet noise with total concentration 10. Visit targets have temperature 1. Moves sample those targets for the first 20 generated plies, then take highest visits. Evaluation uses no noise and highest visits from the first move.

Fresh starts are 12-ply prefixes from distinct archive games. Exclude source games and canonical start positions from the entire scalar corpus and all original, later-RL and move-ranking reserved openings. Independently allocate 20 pilot starts, 1,000 training starts, 40 development pairs, 200 primary final pairs, 40 control pairs and 40 deployment-reference pairs. The runner records allocation hashes before the main study; no historical final scores select models. Naturally recurring positions during generated play are not represented as impossible to encounter.

Pilot: an independent seed and 20 attempted games each at 128/256/512 simulations, with the common initial policy/value model. Require at least 80% completed games, mean root coverage at least 50% on positions with at least four legal moves, fewer than 90% of those positions assigning at least 99% of visits to a single move, and peak process RSS at most 4 GB. These are feasibility/degeneracy gates, not proof of strong self-play. Prefer the highest qualifying simulation budget. If none qualifies, publish pilot rejection and do not launch main training or relax thresholds. A subsequent change requires a distinct prospective protocol. Pilot games do not train the model.

Before the pilot, report a rough forecast from a short disposable benchmark. After the pilot, report a full forecast with 50% margin, including all six fits, generation, 960 development games, 2,160 final games and update/audit overhead. No artificial local runtime cap; CPU only, one numerical-library thread, sequential fits, C$0 paid resources. Measured timing and actual elapsed time are recorded separately from forecasts.

Main run: ten batches of 100 attempted games per treatment/seed. Actor frozen per batch; partial games restart deterministically from their game seed after interruption. Save every completed game atomically before proceeding. Omit opening-prefix positions; truncated games contribute neither policy nor value targets. Require 80 completed games in each batch or stop with an explicit quality failure. Sample exactly 32 positions with replacement per completed game, providing uniform game weight. One shuffled training epoch per batch, batch size 256, AdamW LR 1e-4 and zero weight decay. No replay buffer or treatment-specific replacement games. CPU resume restores model, optimizer, minibatch cursor and all RNG state.

Development: select between batches 5 and 10 using 40 shared color-swapped opening pairs against the frozen initial policy/value MCTS player at the selected simulation budget. Maximize match score; ties prefer batch 5. Lock all six selections before any final matches. Seed 42 is a predeclared illustration, not the best seed.

Primary final: 200 fresh color-swapped pairs per seed, anchored against baseline at equal simulations. Secondary controls: both treatments against initial MCTS on a separate 40-pair block, and both against the unchanged released default on another 40-pair block at 100 ms/move. The alpha-beta reference includes quiescence in its node count and wall-clock budget; MCTS simulation counts are not alpha-beta node counts. All encoding/inference is timed. No Stockfish reference is needed. Time-budget runs are resumable by completed game but not guaranteed bitwise repeatable.

Maximum 512 generated plies in training and matches. Training caps have no fabricated target. Evaluation caps are explicitly provisional half-point scores, with cap counts, uncapped-pair analysis and worst/best score bounds. Final statistics bootstrap shared complete opening pairs jointly across fixed seeds, separately reporting seed SD. A positive primary result requires aggregate lower 95% bound above 50% and at least two seeds with point score above 50%; it does not authorize model release. All controls are secondary. Record search work, move latency, throughput, truncations, peak RAM and cost. Smaller drift or outcome loss does not establish stronger play.

Completion requires every planned fit, development selection, final comparison, audit and report; pilot rejection or partial execution is named explicitly. Preserve negative results. Do not tune 0.1 or expand the search after results. The original pure outcome RL and off-policy coefficient-1 replay diagnosis remain separate studies.
