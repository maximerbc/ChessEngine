Interpretation and limitations

The loss is −log softmax of the teacher move among legal candidates. Lower cross-entropy means better imitation, not necessarily stronger play. The teacher supplies one preferred move even when alternatives are similar. Ranking probabilities are not win probabilities. All validation selection is development evidence; final results describe only the frozen seed and search protocol. Equal-time results depend on this CPU and its load.
