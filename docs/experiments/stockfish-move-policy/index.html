Stockfish move imitation

A CNN scores every legal move using feature-v3 board state and player-relative move coordinates. It learns first-PV Stockfish move labels on exactly 800,000 training, 100,000 validation and 100,000 test positions. All weights started fresh. This differs from learning scalar Stockfish position assessments or moves actually played by humans.

The fixed seed-42 validation-selected checkpoint is playable experimentally through direct legal-move inference, without alpha-beta, a value head, or Stockfish choosing its moves during play. Probabilities are legal-move preferences, not winning chances.
