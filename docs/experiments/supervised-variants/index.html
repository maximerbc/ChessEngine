Playable supervised variants

One fixed seed-42 representative per trained configuration. Classical is an additional untrained baseline. Identical trained weights are listed once. Gameplay does not train models.

The existing Supervised default and checkpoint remain unchanged. All additional variants are experimental: availability is not qualification or evidence of stronger play. Historical failed release gates and negative results remain in their original reports.

Value networks use the same bounded alpha-beta search. Stockfish move imitation and human-game prediction choose a legal move directly, without search or a position-value head. Move probabilities are not win probabilities. The human CNN retains its original 13-plane encoder; unlike feature-v1/v2/v3 value encoders, it does not encode all historical state. Rules and draw claims still use the complete history.

The 180k model missed its promotion gate. The combined model's release comparison also failed. Weight averaging missed the 2% adoption threshold. Stockfish-policy seed 43 collapsed and failed export parity; seed 42 alone represents that configuration here. These outcomes have not been rewritten.
