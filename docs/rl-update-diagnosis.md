# RL regression: fixed-trajectory update diagnosis

Frozen before execution on 2026-10-13. This is a new development experiment, following the completed negative RL study. It does not replace or reinterpret its final evidence.

## Evidence motivating the intervention

The completed RL study passed independent outcome/sign/history/actor audits, but mean completed self-play search depth was only 1.40–1.42. Its selected models agreed less with Stockfish and lost against the initializer. These observations are compatible with noisy shallow-play returns and loss of useful initialization knowledge; they do not identify a single cause. Monte Carlo prediction estimates returns under the generating policy, not optimal chess values (Sutton and Barto, chapter 5: https://www.incompleteideas.net/book/bookdraft2018mar21.pdf). This value-only/shallow-search system is not AlphaZero's policy-and-value/MCTS algorithm (https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01815).

## Controlled comparison

Use only saved self-play training games from the three original seeds. Replay complete PGN histories, omit opening-prefix states, exclude truncated games, and deterministically sample 32 positions per completed game with replacement. All treatments for a seed receive exactly the same sampled positions, outcomes and minibatch permutations. The same seed-42 supervised model initializes every fit. No new Stockfish labels, final-match outcomes or released models enter training.

Compare exactly three treatments, on seeds 42/43/44, with ten sequential batches:

- Reference: AdamW LR 1e-4, outcome MSE, no weight decay.
- Smaller step: LR 1e-5, otherwise reference.
- Anchored: LR 1e-4, outcome MSE plus mean squared deviation from the frozen initializer's predictions, coefficient 1.

The anchored objective is `mean((v-z)^2) + lambda * mean((v-v_initial)^2)`. Initial predictions are detached. For an unconstrained individual prediction its minimizer is `(z + lambda*v_initial)/(1+lambda)`: this deliberately trades return fitting against preserving initial estimates. It is a separate regularized treatment, not the original outcome-only RL experiment.

The saved trajectories were generated by the old actors. These fits are **off-policy fixed-data ablations**, not new on-policy self-play runs or evidence of policy improvement. The reference need not reproduce the historical RL trajectory because the sample draws are newly controlled; it is the matched reference for this experiment. All treatments use the final tenth checkpoint, fixed in advance, with no batch selection.

## Evaluation and decision

Run every candidate against the same frozen initializer on all 40 existing development pairs at 128 nodes and quiescence depth 2. Compare mean score against the matched replay reference, every seed separately, and jointly paired openings across seeds. Call a treatment worth an online follow-up only if its mean development score exceeds reference by at least 2 percentage points and improves at least two seeds. This is a screening rule, not statistical proof. Rank by mean development score; ties prefer the smaller-step treatment.

Teacher-validation MSE, prediction spread, deviation from the initializer and before/after outcome MSE are explanatory diagnostics, not candidate selectors. No old or new final games are run here. Further confirmation requires separate fresh openings and a live self-play study of any candidate that passes the screen. Do not automatically deploy a candidate.

## First executed diagnostics

All nine fits completed. Reference mean teacher-validation MSE is about 0.30374, compared with 0.24158 for the initializer. Smaller-step fits average about 0.24406; anchored fits average about 0.26526. All reference seeds reduce same-batch outcome loss while moving away from the initializer. The baseline validation prediction SD is 0.683; reference fits shrink it to approximately 0.50–0.51. This is measurable prediction drift and range compression, not proof that every changed prediction is worse.

Among equally weighted sampled self-play states where the initializer has absolute value at least 0.75, about 22–24% subsequently draw and 6% finish with the opposite outcome sign. The game outcomes are valid: this demonstrates objective mismatch, not mislabeled games. There are 233–239 unique openings and 989–993 distinct PGNs per seed, so exact duplicated games alone do not explain the regression.

Development matches are in progress. Neither preserving teacher agreement nor lowering replay outcome loss proves stronger play. No candidate is promoted before the predeclared playing-strength screen, and passing that screen only motivates a separate live self-play study.
