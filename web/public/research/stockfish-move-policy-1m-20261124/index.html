Learning the teacher’s preferred move

A completed three-seed CNN study used 800,000 training, 100,000 validation and 100,000 test positions, with separately generated Stockfish move labels. This is an earlier experiment than the playable elite-human CNN.

Seed 43 collapsed to constant move scores. It retained a maximum probability discrepancy of 0.1429 in the corrected CPU export check. The cause of the optimization collapse was not established. Its failure remains part of the result; another seed was not substituted after viewing test outcomes.

Mean test agreement across all seeds was 23.33%, with a sample standard deviation of 14.28 percentage points. Agreement with a bounded teacher does not establish playing strength, Elo or win probability. No model from this study is playable.
