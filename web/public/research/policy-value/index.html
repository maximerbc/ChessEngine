Policy/value self-play and prediction anchoring

Status: pilot_in_progress. Local CPU; C$0 paid resources. No model promotion.

Two treatments share a supervised-initialized value network and fresh policy head: visit-policy/outcome learning, and the same objective plus a 0.1 penalty toward frozen initial value predictions. This is an adapted AlphaZero-inspired study, with batch-frozen actors and a compact MLP. Original AlphaZero paper.

Methods and limits

Actor frozen per batch; no new teacher labels; exactly 32 sampled states per completed game; truncated games excluded from both losses. Three seeds share one initialization. All six development selections precede final games. Bootstrap shared color-swapped opening pairs jointly across fixed seeds; seed variation is reported separately. No Elo or calibrated win-probability claim. Lower drift and a head-to-head advantage can still mean less regression rather than improvement over initialization.
