Does the 180k model qualify for release?

It did not pass the development gate. The original 80,299-position model remains the demo default. The proposed replacement is the existing seed-42, feature-v1, 128/64 MLP trained on 180,302 positions. Its inference weights are preserved as a candidate, unchanged from the saved training checkpoint.

Why this candidate?

The expanded-data study reduced mean validation MSE from 0.30132 to 0.27350 across three seeds (9.2%). The selected seed-42 candidate's validation MSE is 0.27411. These are measures of agreement with Stockfish position assessments, not move imitation or calibrated winning probabilities. The data comparison did not match optimizer update counts, and repeated validation use makes it development evidence.

Direct check against the previous release

Seed 42 was fixed before measurement. All 40 opening pairs swap colors and come from the development pool excluding the expanded supervised corpus and old reserved games/positions. Each side receives 100 ms per move, a 1,000,000,000-node ceiling, qdepth two, automatic draw claims, fresh search tables and a 512-ply cap. Two local workers ran the matches at C$0 paid cost. Every saved game was independently replayed to verify legal history and outcome.

The frozen gate requires all 80 games, score at least 50%, at most 5% capped games and uncapped-pair score at least 50%. This candidate missed the score gate. The interval's estimand is the paired-opening score for these fixed models; it does not include training-seed or checkpoint-selection uncertainty.

What this check does and does not establish

This is a bounded development screen. Its uncertainty conclusion is inconclusive. A passing point-score gate is not proof of stronger chess play. Production remains at 10,000 nodes, 1,500 ms and qdepth four; those settings were not evaluated by this release screen. No fresh final strength comparison, new training, model ensemble or deployment is claimed.

The 48.75% point score is below the 50% threshold frozen before measurement. Its broad interval does not establish a regression either. We do not try another seed, change the threshold or rerun until the candidate passes.

The later feature-v3 combined evaluator that failed its own release check is a different checkpoint. Original local-study charts and recorded games remain historical evidence for the old release. Existing evolutionary protocols retain their old supervised hashes and cannot silently resume using this new model.
