What limits the supervised evaluator?

A frozen-model audit, nine data-size experiments, and a predefined conditional regularization comparison. This report evaluates approximation to Stockfish targets—not chess strength.

The public demo and released checkpoints are unchanged. All results use existing training/validation data. No final-test results entered selection.

Findings and next steps

  1. Test broader training coverage next: the largest training subset has lower mean validation MSE than the smallest. More data also received more optimizer updates, so this is evidence for the combined training procedure rather than a fixed-compute causal effect.
  2. Do not increase regularization based on these results alone. Audit teacher-label stability and position coverage before changing capacity or launching a larger search.
  3. Audit deeper Stockfish labels on a fixed development sample before mass relabelling. Large residuals alone cannot distinguish a noisy teacher, insufficient representation, optimization failure, or an unfamiliar position.

Reproducibility and limitations

Training/validation epoch work: 51.7 seconds. Study invocation wall time: 165.5 seconds. Paid compute: C$0. Per-run wall times describe completed invocations and exclude any interrupted attempts; epoch work excludes encoding, serialization and report generation.

Targets are finite-search Stockfish assessments, not ground truth or observed win probabilities. Validation has been used repeatedly for early stopping and development. Group analysis is exploratory and multiple comparisons are not adjusted. Seed SD and game-bootstrap uncertainty measure different sources of variation. Independent final positions and controlled matches are required before claiming generalization or strength improvements. Raw data, predictions and intermediate checkpoints remain local.
