Self-play: results and lessons

Self-play did not establish an improvement; the larger follow-up regressed. These models are retired from live play. This archive preserves what we tried, measured and learned.

  • Original local study: inconclusive improvement across three seeds. Charts and recorded-match replay remain in the original-study archive; recorded replay does not invoke a live engine.
  • Larger live self-play follow-up: 44.125% mean final score against its initializer; a regression, using different checkpoints.
  • Fixed-trajectory diagnosis: prediction anchoring passed a development screen. This was offline replay, not successful live reinforcement learning.
  • Policy/value feasibility: a completed 60-attempt pilot; the full comparison was deferred and provides no strength conclusion.

What changed next

Shallow-play returns and prediction drift motivated a separately preregistered evolutionary experiment. We preserve the negative evidence rather than calling it a successful model.
